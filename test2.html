How can scanning a QR code be dangerous?

Scammers can replace a real QR code with a fake one that redirects you to a convincing website. It may look legitimate but can be used to steal your login details, personal information, or bank details.


Common Examples

  • Parking lots: You might stumble across a QR code to pay for parking, but it opens a fake payment page designed to steal your card details.
  • Restaurant tables: Threat actors might place a fake menu QR code in restaurants that redirects you to a fake ordering page designed to collect your information.
  • Cafés: We often see QR codes to connect to free Wi-Fi in public areas, but this might just be a trap to gather your personal information.
  • Public posters or giveaways: There could be a QR code offering a prize or discount, but instead it leads to a credential-harvesting website, and you win nothing.
Remember: Always think before you take any action.
